# Dartmouth Energy Twin

> **Visualizing the invisible infrastructure powering Dartmouth College.**

Dartmouth Energy Twin is an interactive 3D digital twin that models Dartmouth College's campus and its transition toward a cleaner, more sustainable energy future.

## Vision

Every day thousands of students, faculty, and visitors walk across Dartmouth's campus without realizing an entire network of energy infrastructure operates beneath their feet.

This project makes those invisible systems visible through immersive 3D visualization, simulation, and interactive exploration.

## Project Goals

- Create a complete interactive 3D model of Dartmouth College.
- Visualize the campus energy network.
- Educate users about sustainable infrastructure.
- Demonstrate modern digital twin technologies.

## Core Features

- Interactive 3D campus
- Smart buildings with metadata
- Underground infrastructure visualization
- Animated energy flow
- Renewable energy layers
- Time and seasonal simulation
- Campus analytics dashboard
- AI campus energy assistant (future)

# Development Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Campus Foundation
**Goal:** Build the complete 3D representation of Dartmouth.

### Tools
- Autodesk Maya
- Google Maps/Earth reference imagery
- CesiumJS
- Next.js

### Deliverables
- Terrain model
- Roads and sidewalks
- Academic, residential, and athletic buildings (LOD1)
- Trees and landscape assets
- Export optimized models (glTF)
- Import into CesiumJS
- Basic navigation and camera controls

---

## Phase 2 — Smart Campus
**Goal:** Turn every building into an interactive digital object.

### Tools
- React
- TypeScript
- Supabase
- PostgreSQL

### Deliverables
- Building metadata database
- Search and filtering
- Fly-to animation
- Information side panel
- Photos and accessibility information
- Building categorization

---

## Phase 3 — Energy Network
